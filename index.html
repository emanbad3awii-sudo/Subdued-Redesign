About Subdued

Subdued is a brand created for independent, expressive, and naturally confident girls. Founded in 1994, the brand has always drawn inspiration from the everyday lives and aesthetics of teenage girls. Their world continues to shape every collection. Rooted in Italian heritage, Subdued expresses a refined and understated approach to fashion, where simplicity, quality, and attention to detail define each piece. The result is a wardrobe that feels effortless, personal, and closely connected to real life.
